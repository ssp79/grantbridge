# Outside sources cited in the report and slides

Each source was read on the date shown. A quotation is copied from the page; nothing here is paraphrased as a quotation.

## How small most nonprofits are

- **Publisher:** National Council of Nonprofits
- **Page:** Nonprofit Impact Matters, https://www.nonprofitimpactmatters.org/
- **Accessed:** 2026-10-21 (read twice that day; the wording was the same both times)
- **Quotation:** "92% spend less than $1 million annually; 88% spend less than $500,000."
- **Date of the figure:** the page gives no data year. The report it promotes, *Nonprofit Impact Matters*, is dated September 2019 (from its PDF file name). The report and slides cite it as the council's 2019 report.
- **Used in:** report section 2 (Motivation) and slide 2.

## Grant payments, matching, and cash flow

**1. Urban Institute (Sarah L. Pettijohn and Elizabeth T. Boris, with Carol J. De Vita and Saunji D. Fyffe), *Nonprofit-Government Contracts and Grants: Findings from the 2013 National Survey*, December 2013.**

- **File:** https://www.urban.org/sites/default/files/publication/24231/412962-Nonprofit-Government-Contracts-and-Grants-Findings-from-the-National-Survey.PDF
- **Accessed:** 2026-10-21. SHA-256 of the PDF read: `6cb2dbf4e61f095fd8d2e9b5710333079f6aed5aaeda5bc8bea0b4f7b77bf166`.
- **Quotation, PDF page 36:** "In 2012, 45 percent of nonprofits with government contracts or grants reported problems with governments making payments on their legal commitments later than the contract specifications."
- **Quotation, PDF page 23:** "Government contracts and grants often require nonprofits to match or share a portion of their contracts or grants with donations or other types of funding."
- **Date of the figure:** the survey covers 2012. It is the most recent national figure we found, and it is old.

**2. Tennessee Nonprofit Network, *Making Grants Work for Nonprofits in Tennessee* (final report), July 2024.**

- **File:** https://tnnonprofits.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Final-Report-Making-Grants-Work-for-Nonprofits-in-Tennessee.pdf
- **Accessed:** 2026-10-21. SHA-256 of the PDF read: `926176f45f1932ec0a96e18f9cee88fabae4e847a363f9a877e2bd58e5bb904b`.
- **Quotation, PDF page 9:** "Delayed reimbursements create a crippling financial burden for nonprofits. Many lack the reserves to cover operating expenses and staff salaries while they wait for payment."
- **Scope:** state grants in one state. The report and slides say so.

**3. National Council of Nonprofits, "Common Problems in Government-Nonprofit Grants and Contracts".**

- **Page:** https://www.councilofnonprofits.org/trends-and-policy-issues/state-policy-tax-law/common-problems-government-nonprofit-grants-and
- **Accessed:** 2026-10-21.
- **Quotation:** "Late payments by governments is a problem that is both frequent (45% of nonprofits responding) and debilitating". Its 45% is the Urban Institute survey's figure above, so it is not a second, independent figure.

- **Used in:** report section 2 (Motivation) and slide 2.
- **How they were checked:** each PDF was downloaded from the address above, its hash recorded, and the quotation found in the text of the page given. The council's page was checked in its HTML.
